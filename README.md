A collection of the houserules I've made to the PF2e system, easily implemented in foundry so I can brewrata the changes to any world I please.

# Tori's Unarmed Houserule
Most people are not trained in hand to hand combat, and using a weapon of any kind will always be considered more effective than using your bare hands. Unless you are wearing Handwraps of Mighty Blows, have the Powerful Fist feature, OR are granted special permission by the GM, all basic unarmed attacks count as being improvised weapon attacks, and take a -2 to attack rolls.

# Tori's Feat Fix

Everyone gets a level 1 general feat.

Everyone gets ancestry feats every odd level, much like Ancestry paragon, but without the extra Level 1 feat.

# Buffs to effects

Buff disarm: the -2 penalty to attack is changed to a -4. Further attempts disarm gaint a stacking +2 untyped bonus.

Buff grabbed: untyped -1 to ac

Buff paralyzed: lose your Dexterity Modifier to ac

Buff prone: untyped -1 to ac

Buff restrained: untyped -2 to ac

# Changes to Classes

All full spellcasters get disjointed upgrades to spell attack proficiency 2 earlier than they would normally in order to match with other martials getting weapon expertise at those levels, spell DC proficiency remains the same.

Every instance of a class feature upgrading a success to a critical success on any saving throw has now been changed to the fortune effect of rolling twice and keeping the higher result.

Thaumaturge:
- may choose any mental attribute as their key attribute (including spellcasting)

Oracle:
- may choose Wisdom or Charisma as their key attribute (including spellcasting)

Sorcerer:
- may choose any mental attribute as their key attribute (including spellcasting)

Summoner:
- may choose Wisdom or Charisma as their key attribute (including spellcasting)

Kineticist:
- may choose any attribute as their key attribute (including spellcasting)

Guardian:
- may choose Constitution or Strength as their key attribute.

Commander:
- may choose Charisma or Intelligence as their key attribute.
